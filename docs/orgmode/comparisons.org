#+TITLE: Head-to-head
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

Head-to-head results that are already in this tree. Each link is the
page that records the numbers. The suite tests named here are the ones
that recompute them.

* OVITO

[[file:explanation/ovito-findrings.org][OVITO FindRings]] is a subset of
the primitive-ring census. On K2,3, d-SEAMS reports three chordless
4-rings and FindRings reports two. On the 864-atom interface frame at
3.5 Å every d-SEAMS ring through size 10 is primitive, and each OVITO
bin is no larger than that census. ~meson test ovito_ring_counts~ and
the K2,3 case in ~tests/test_franzblau_extra.cpp~ recompute both.

OVITO 3.15 has no Steinhardt modifier. Local \(q_4\) and \(q_6\), and
the Lechner-Dellago averages, are compared with freud and pyscal3
instead. ~meson test q4_q6_equal~ runs that comparison.

CHILL+ and the neighbour list against OVITO are rows of the
[[file:explanation/performance-h2h.org][performance tables]].

* freud

~meson test q4_q6_equal~ compares per-atom \(q_4\) and \(q_6\), the
Lechner-Dellago averages, and the mean of each, on ideal ice Ih, ideal
ice Ic, and one liquid. The neighbour rule is the same in all three
codes: a pair counts when its minimum-image distance is strictly below
3.5 Å. pyscal3 matches to \(10^{-10}\). freud stores \(q_l\) as
float32, so a crystal agrees to a few units in the last place and a
disordered shell keeps a larger residual. An empty shell is 0 in
d-SEAMS and NaN in freud and pyscal3. The
[[file:howto/q4-q6.org][q4 and q6 how-to]] records why that 0 stays:
the empty accumulator is the result, and a NaN switch would enter
every mean and every CHILL+ label.

The same cutoff \(q_6\) on the large sitting, and the published
literature numbers, are in the
[[file:explanation/performance-h2h.org][performance tables]] and
[[file:howto/literature-reproduce.org][the literature reproduction]].

* SQQ

SQQ ring times are a row of the
[[file:explanation/performance-h2h.org][performance tables]] and of
[[file:howto/literature-h2h.org][the literature codes]]. The benchmark
CSVs under ~benchmarks/~ are the source of those cells.
~docs/render_h2h.py --check~ fails when a cell is missing from its CSV.

* PLUMED

The colvar is [[file:howto/engine-plumed.org][the PLUMED engine]]. Its
\(q_6\) on the large sitting is a row of the
[[file:explanation/performance-h2h.org][performance tables]]: on the
liquid the gap is the rational switch, not the hard cutoff. The
literature reproduction records the same comparison next to freud.
~meson test docs_engine_plumed~ runs the engine page.
