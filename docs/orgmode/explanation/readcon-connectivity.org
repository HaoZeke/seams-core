#+TITLE: Atom identity on a nucleation window
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

A cutoff list, a primitive ring and a CHILL+ label are properties of
an atom. Following them from one frame to the next needs the atom id,
and the cutoff list itself needs the cell. An eOn =.con= frame written
by readcon keeps both on every frame. An XYZ frame does not: the id
seams stores is the line order, and the cell has to be carried beside
the coordinates.

* The window

The trajectory is one 4096-molecule mW liquid from the homogeneous
quench deck, seed 20261118. The thermostat is the deck's: a 10 fs
step, 10000 steps of NVT at 350 K (damping 200 fs), then NVT at 180 K
with the same damping. The saved window is the 81 frames from 659 ps to 739 ps after the
quench (1 ps apart). The deck's =nskip= leaves the step on a dump
multiple, so that step is included. The published crossing of a
50-molecule CHILL+ core (705 ps for this seed) sits inside the file. The
original dump is not in the tree, so this window was regenerated with
that deck and that LAMMPS tag (22 Jul 2025, Update 6).

* What is compared

The cutoff is 3.5 Å. Primitive rings are the Franzblau rings of that
graph through seven members. The walk numbers vertices by index, so
the atoms are ordered by coordinates before the search and each
vertex keeps its atom id. CHILL+ is the published rule, not the
OVITO rule. Each XYZ frame is the same coordinates in an order drawn
from =mt19937= seeded with =20261118 + frame * 2654435769=. The join
uses the atom id. For =.con= that id is the LAMMPS id. For XYZ it is
the line number in that frame.

Neighbour-pair persistence is the fraction of cutoff pairs at frame
=t= that are still pairs at =t+tau=. Six-ring persistence is the
fraction of atoms that sit in a six-ring at =t= and still sit in one
at =t+tau=. Ice-type agreement is the fraction of atoms whose
published CHILL+ label is unchanged. The per-frame ring counts and the
ice-type counts do not use the id, so they match between the two
files. Only the join changes. Nearly every molecule in this window
already sits in a six-ring, so that fraction stays high after the
shuffle. The neighbour pairs fall to about one in a thousand, and the
CHILL+ label falls to the chance rate of the liquid majority.

readcon-db ingests the =.con= trajectory with =sinp::ingestConCorpus=
and reads it back by the formula =O:4096= with =sinp::readConCorpus=.
The decoded positions, ids and cell match the file. The timing is
eight repeats on the core count recorded in the result file.
