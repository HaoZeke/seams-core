#+TITLE: TRACE and signature cages
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

TRACE (Hsu and Lin, J. Chem. Theory Comput. 2025,
[[https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.5c01459][10.1021/acs.jctc.5c01459]])
assembles hydrate cages from a hydrogen-bond graph. d-SEAMS answers
the oxygen question: which rings tile a closed polyhedron of a given
face census. The timed comparison is the how-to
[[file:../howto/cages-trace.org][Cages and TRACE]]. This page is the
two constructions.

* The hydrogen-bond cage

TRACE places one hydrogen \(0.96\,\text{Å}\) from each oxygen along each
of the two nearest oxygen-oxygen bonds, then keeps a bond when the
donor-acceptor distance is inside \(0.35\,\text{nm}\) and the angle at
the donor is within \(35^\circ\) of linear. Cups are grown by depth-first
search and closed when the face list matches a hydrate polyhedron.
The three census strings used in the sitting are \(5^{12}\),
\(5^{12}6^{2}\) and \(5^{12}6^{4}\).

d-SEAMS does not place those hydrogens and does not call TRACE. On ice,
which has no five-rings, both searches report no cage of those three
kinds. The numbers are in the rc3 sitting tables.

* A closed face list

~seams_frame_signature~ and ~seams cages --signature~ count a set of
primitive rings as one cage when two conditions hold. Every vertex of
the surface lies on at least three faces that close around it, and the
cell is a topological sphere:

\[
V - E + F = 2.
\]

\(F\) is the number of faces, \(E\) the number of distinct edges, and
\(V\) the number of distinct vertices. A ring whose minimum-image bonds
sum to a nonzero lattice vector winds through the periodic cell and is
dropped before this test. The default face-edge window is
\([2.65, 3.15]\) in the coordinate units of the file. ~none~ leaves the
bound to the census alone.

The string ~512~ is twelve pentagons. ~51262~ is twelve pentagons and
two hexagons. The C entry that takes the window is
~seams_frame_signature_edge~.
