#+TITLE: SQQ
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

SQQ against d-SEAMS. The census and the ratios are written from the
vendored CSVs at build time. A timing row names the version, the
thread count, the repeat count and the core count. On the TIP4P/2005
frame the edges and the rings are the exact-match rows. The chordless
census is the like-for-like ratio. CHILL+ against SQQ's ice stage is
the whole-tool ratio.
