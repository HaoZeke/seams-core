#+TITLE: Carried rings and the hop ball
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

A trajectory frame shares almost every bond with the frame before it.
~primitive::RingUpdater~ recomputes only the rings that can have
changed, and the result equals ~primitive::ringNetwork~ on that frame.
The measured speedups are the how-to
[[file:../howto/linkcell-carried.org][Linkcell neighbours and carried updates]].

* What can change

Let \(G\) and \(G'\) be the neighbour graphs of two consecutive frames,
and let \(C\) be the vertices whose adjacency row differs. Those
vertices are the endpoints of every added or removed edge. Write \(D\)
for the deepest ring the search keeps, and

\[
L = \lfloor D/2 \rfloor, \qquad
r = \lfloor (L - 2)/2 \rfloor.
\]

A cycle of at most \(D\) members is a primitive ring of one graph and
not of the other only if it contains a changed edge, or a changed edge
lies on a shortcut between two of its members. One endpoint of that
shortcut then lies within \(r\) edges of a ring member. Every ring that
differs therefore meets \(T\), the set of vertices within distance \(r\)
of \(C\) in the union of \(G\) and \(G'\).

For \(D \le 7\), \(r = 0\), so \(T = C\). The update drops the stored
rings that meet \(T\) and adds the rings of \(G'\) that meet \(T\). Each
new ring is enumerated once, from its lowest-indexed member in \(T\).
Rings stay in the canonical order of ~ringNetwork~, so the lists compare
equal element by element.

The balls used by the longer primitive test are refreshed only when an
edge within \(L - 1\) of a vertex changes. ~--rebuild~, a single frame,
and a worker pool above one thread turn the carry off and call
~ringNetwork~ on every frame.

When the carry stays on, the enumeration of the changed sources uses
~sourceTeam~. It stays on the calling thread until those sources fill
the workers, about 80 sources for each thread. A few hundred changed
vertices are still one thread. That width is not ~teamFor~, which
would also put a crystal of a few hundred molecules on one thread. The
timed carried update is the hop kernel on
[[file:trace-threads.org][the thread-control page]].

~seams_frame_chill_rings~ is the language call that can keep a neighbour
list across frames when a skin is set. The ring carry itself is the
updater above, used by a sequential ~seams~ frame walk.
