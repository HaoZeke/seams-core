Two warm-ups, then ten samples.

One q4 sample in an earlier eight-sample run took 523.6 ms.
The first call of a degree is the one that faults.
On this rerun that call took 508.1 ms and touched 57547 pages.
Every later q4 call touched none.
The ten steady samples are min 290.36, median 290.91, interquartile range 7.44, max 310.44.
The =q_lm= scratch is kept and reused, so that cost stays on the first call, which the warm-ups drop.
One q6 sample in the same session reached 462.98 ms and faulted no pages.
Sixty further q6 calls, pinned the same way, stayed between 299.9 and 324.6 ms.
Each of those ran on the CPU.
The scheduler delay on every one of them was under 0.05 ms.
=discovery/v13-q6-sched.txt= is that probe.
The 523.6 ms point did not come back, and the one q6 outlier is not a page fault, not a fresh allocation, and not a repeatable first touch.

The one-thread q4-bar median here is 368.01 (interquartile range 3.13, samples 356.35–387.92).
The parent =e943930a=, in the same session, is 363.82 (interquartile range 12.51, samples 353.29–371.97).
The medians differ by 4.2 ms, and each median sits inside the other build's sample range.
An earlier gap of 369.8 against 354.0 is that spread. It is not a slower average.

* Production-stride ring update

Both tables are 4096 molecules, 200 transitions, eight cores and eight
OpenMP threads. One warm-up, then eight repetitions. The median is
type-7. The carry time is the hop-ball update. The rebuild time is a
full search of the same frame. Speedup is rebuild divided by carry.
Every transition stayed inside the hop ball, and the carried rings
matched the full search. The logs name no machine.

The mW window is the growth trajectory at 208 K. The step is 10 fs, so

* Release candidate sitting

Neighbours, local q4 and q6, CHILL+, primitive rings, the shell
symmetry label and GenIce cages were retimed in one sitting, at
110592 and 884736 molecules, on one thread and on eight. Six calls
after a warm-up. The host is an eight-core Intel Xeon, family 6
model 207, and the name is not recorded. The table, the versions
and the rows that do not match an earlier quoted time are in
[[file:literature-h2h.org][Literature head-to-head]]. The raw lines are
=benchmarks/rc3-sitting.csv= and =benchmarks/rc3-sitting.txt=.
=snakemake rc3_sitting= regenerates them.

The 20.3 ms and 50.7 ms vesin times are the same full list of
442368 pairs. =n_threads=0= follows =OMP_NUM_THREADS=, which is 1
in this environment, so the pool stays one thread and the median
is 50.6 ms. An explicit pool of eight threads is 20.3 ms.
=benchmarks/rc3-vesin-gap.txt= is that probe. A half list is a
different pair count. The cell list, the copy and the layout move
the eight-thread time by a few milliseconds.
