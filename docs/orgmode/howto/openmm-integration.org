#+TITLE: OpenMM
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

Run neighbours, primitive rings, CHILL, and CHILL+ while OpenMM steps.
The reporter calls ~libseams_lang~ on the host and appends the analysed
atoms to a LAMMPS dump. ~seams~ on that dump prints the same lines.
The package is ~adapters/python/dseams_insitu~. A short note of the
same call is ~extras/openmm/README.md~.

* Call

Put ~adapters/python~ on ~PYTHONPATH~. Point ~SEAMS_LIB~ at
~libseams_lang.so~ from the Meson build
(~bbdir/bindings/libseams_lang.so~).

#+begin_src python
from dseams_insitu import DseamsReporter, Engine

with Engine(library, "census.txt", cutoff=3.5) as eng:
    sim.reporters.append(
        DseamsReporter(eng, "frames.lammpstrj", 10, indices=oxygen)
    )
    sim.step(100)
#+end_src

~indices~ selects the oxygen atoms of TIP4P/Ew. A one-site model omits
~indices~. Positions come from the context in angstrom, with the
periodic box enforced. The second argument of ~DseamsReporter~ is the
dump. The third is the stride in steps. The cutoff is angstrom. The
bond graph is the cutoff list, the default of ~seams chill~.

* Match

The dump stores the analysed atoms as LAMMPS type 1. Snapshot numbers
for ~--frame~ start at 1, in the order the reporter wrote them.
~NO_COLOR=1~ leaves the ice names uncoloured. ~--jobs 1~ runs one frame
worker.

#+begin_src bash
seams neighbours frames.lammpstrj --cutoff 3.5 --type 1 --graph cutoff --frame 1 --jobs 1
seams rings frames.lammpstrj --cutoff 3.5 --type 1 --graph cutoff --frame 1 --jobs 1
seams chill frames.lammpstrj --cutoff 3.5 --type 1 --graph cutoff --frame 1 --jobs 1
seams chill-plus frames.lammpstrj --cutoff 3.5 --type 1 --graph cutoff --frame 1 --jobs 1
#+end_src

~seams neighbours~ is the neighbour block in the census, including the
~neighbours~ and ~end_neighbours~ lines. Neighbour ids on a row are
sorted. ~seams rings~ is the ~rings 3:a 4:b 5:c 6:d 7:e 8:f~ line
(primitive rings, depth 8). ~seams chill~ is the text after the census
prefix ~chill~. ~seams chill-plus~ is the text after ~chill-plus~.

~tests/openmm/test_reporter.py~ builds both boxes when it runs. The
2 nm box (251 waters) is checked at steps 50 and 100. The 4.93 nm box
(3910 waters) is checked at steps 100 and 200. On each of those frames
the in-situ rings line and the CHILL+ line equal ~seams rings~ and
~seams chill-plus~. The neighbour list and CHILL match as well. The
dumps are temporary. See ~data/README~. OpenMM comes from pip.

* Overhead

~tests/openmm/bench_overhead.py~ times plain ~Simulation.step~ and the
same call with the reporter. The stride is the third argument of
~DseamsReporter~: 10, 100, or 1000 steps. Each timed leg is 1000 steps
of 2 fs, so those strides analyse 100, 10, and 1 frames. Build and
minimisation sit outside the timer. One warmup of each leg is discarded
for each box. The sample is six paired trials, run one after another.
The overhead of a trial is the in-situ time minus the plain time. The
IQR is the Tukey hinge: the median of the upper half minus the median
of the lower half.

| waters | interval | plain median (s) | plain IQR (s) | in-situ median (s) | in-situ IQR (s) | overhead median (s) | overhead IQR (s) |

| 251 | 100 | 3.296226 | 0.027771 | 3.448135 | 0.041311 | 0.143138 | 0.052764 |
| 251 | 1000 | 3.297198 | 0.011827 | 3.333070 | 0.038383 | 0.028786 | 0.054455 |
| 3910 | 10 | 41.822152 | 0.671093 | 62.740334 | 0.264869 | 20.854141 | 0.431330 |
| 3910 | 100 | 41.614041 | 0.290201 | 43.697378 | 0.365814 | 2.162122 | 0.390859 |
| 3910 | 1000 | 41.422865 | 0.139625 | 41.704660 | 0.083812 | 0.282673 | 0.159722 |

The 251-water box is a 2 nm cube. The 3910-water box is a 4.93 nm cube.
Both use TIP4P/Ew, a PME cutoff of 0.9 nm, and Langevin dynamics at
300 K. OpenMM uses its CPU platform with one thread. ~OMP_NUM_THREADS~
is 1.

The host is an Intel Xeon, 8 cores, 1 thread per core, L1d 384 KiB,
L2 16 MiB, L3 320 MiB, 49342816 kB of RAM, Linux 6.12.113+. ~seams~ and
~libseams_lang~ were built with g++ 15.2.0. OpenMM 8.6.1 and NumPy
2.5.4 came from pip on Python 3.13.14.
