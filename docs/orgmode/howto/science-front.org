#+TITLE: Basal profiles and the nucleation census
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

~science-front~ walks a LAMMPS dump with the threaded CHILL+, q6 and
shell kernels from ~science/interface-nucleation~ at ~55192e3d~.
The calls are ~layersOf~, ~nucleusOf~ and ~criticalSize~. This docs
branch does not compile them. ~meson test docs_science_front~ runs
the two commands when the sources are in the build, and exits 77
when they are not.

The critical size is an upper bound, not a committor. It is the mean
size of the largest cluster in the contiguous band around that
cluster's minimum. The band stops where the size exceeds the minimum
by the larger of 5 molecules and 15 percent. A minimum in the first
two frames means the dump starts after the nucleus is already at
least that large. The interval is a percentile bootstrap of the mean.
It is not the probability that the cluster will grow.

* Basal TIP4P/Ice profile

The recorded run is seventeen frames of a restrained ice Ih basal
slab against liquid TIP4P/Ice at 230 K. The dump interval is 23.95 ps.
The basal normal is z. The cutoff is 3.5 Å. Both fronts are defined
on every frame. The thickness grows at 0.001077 Å/ps. Half of that
slope, the mean advance of the two fronts, is 0.000539 Å/ps.

The small frame in this repository is
~input/traj/interface_f0.lammpstrj~, 864 type-1 waters, with the long
edge along z. One frame is enough to write the layer profile:

#+begin_src bash
science-front interface input/traj/interface_f0.lammpstrj --bin 1 --out-dir science-out
#+end_src

~interface-layers.csv~ is one row per bin. ~interface-fronts.csv~
carries the 0.5 crossing and the 10-90 width. A flat crystal leaves
the front undefined.

* The 148-frame mW census

The trajectory is figshare
[[https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.11448702.v1][10.6084/m9.figshare.11448702.v1]],
148 frames of 4096 molecules, md5 ~c28fc209a2854d2ba49e8f9b55accbe8~.
~input/traj/nucleation_three.lammpstrj~ is its first three frames.
The full dump is not committed. Place it at
~data/science-frames/nucleation.lammpstrj~ and run:

#+begin_src bash
science-front nucleation data/science-frames/nucleation.lammpstrj --out-dir science-out
#+end_src

On that trajectory the largest extended cluster (cubic, hexagonal and
interfacial) has its minimum in the first two frames. The critical
size is 153.333 molecules, bootstrap 146 to 164, and ~upper_bound~
is 1. The core cluster, cubic and hexagonal only, is 61.5 molecules
and is also an upper bound. The q6-bar cluster, cut at 0.36, has its
minimum later. Its size is 117 molecules and ~upper_bound~ is 0.
None of these is a committor.

The first three frames of the same dump, which the repository does
carry, are the start of that series. The extended sizes are 177, 146
and 150. The core sizes are 59, 64 and 70.

OVITO's CHILL+ modifier matches the d-SEAMS ovito-chill+ label on
the basal frame and on nucleation frame 2. The published CHILL+ rule
does not: it keeps the 2015 neighbour test and the four-neighbour q3
average. Freud q6 and q6-bar on the same 3.5 Å ball match the kernel.
