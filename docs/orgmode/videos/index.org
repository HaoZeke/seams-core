#+TITLE: Videos
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

Silent picture tracks for the bindings and the tutorials. Each recording
is a terminal session at 1920 by 1080, with a blank band along the bottom
for captions. The files will live on the Hugging Face d-SEAMS dataset.
Until those uploads exist, the player below points at the placeholder URL.
The caption track is the draft VTT, loaded with a HTML track element and
not burned into the picture.

A later mix of narration audio uses ffmpeg with =-c:a aac -aac_coder fast=.
