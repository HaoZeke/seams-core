#+TITLE: Nucleation
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

The 148-frame mW trajectory is figshare
[[https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.11448702.v1][10.6084/m9.figshare.11448702.v1]].
~input/traj/nucleation_three.lammpstrj~ is its first three
frames, timesteps 15677000, 15678000 and 15679000, 468 kB.
~meson test docs_tutorial_nucleation~ labels each frame and grows the
largest crystal cluster. ~meson test docs_frame_tutorials~ runs the
same three frames in CI (~--small~). Without ~--small~, the runner
tries ~d-SEAMS/nucleation-frames~ at the pinned revision and uses this
file when that fetch does not return it.
~scripts/hf_dataset_prepare.py~ packs the dump for that dataset and
does not upload it.

Crystal atoms are cubic, hexagonal, reCubic and reHex. Two crystal
atoms share a cluster when the cutoff list joins them. The cubic and
hexagonal fractions are those counts divided by 4096.

| frame | cubic | hexagonal | largest cluster |
|-------+-------+-----------+-----------------|
| 1 | 47 | 62 | 59 |
| 2 | 50 | 60 | 64 |
| 3 | 44 | 69 | 70 |

A \(q_6\) cutoff would call every high-\(q_6\) molecule ice and would
not separate the 47 cubic molecules from the 62 hexagonal ones. The
cluster is the connected crystal, which on the first frame is 59
molecules, smaller than the cubic-plus-hexagonal count because the
rest sit in other components.
