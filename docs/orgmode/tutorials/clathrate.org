#+TITLE: Classify a clathrate
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

GenIce builds the three clathrate ices without guests. The cage search
is the signature census: closed polyhedra whose faces match a ring-size
list. ~meson test docs_tutorial_clathrate~ runs that search and checks
the counts.

The cells are already in the tree, each well under a megabyte.

| cell | file | molecules | census |
|------+------+-----------+--------|
| sI | ~input/traj/genice_sI.lammpstrj~ | 46 | 2 of \(5^{12}\), 6 of \(5^{12}6^{2}\) |
| sII | ~input/traj/genice_sII.lammpstrj~ | 136 | 16 of \(5^{12}\), 8 of \(5^{12}6^{4}\) |
| sH | ~input/traj/genice_sH.lammpstrj~ | 136 | four unit cells, so 12, 8 and 4 |

The sH unit cell is 3 of \(5^{12}\), 2 of \(4^{3}5^{6}6^{3}\), and 1 of
\(5^{12}6^{8}\). The file is that cell repeated four times, and the
test checks \(12, 8, 4 = 4 \times (3, 2, 1)\).

sH is a skewed cell. The default cage-edge window measures bond lengths
as if the box were orthorhombic, and the long faces then fall outside
it. ~--cage-edge none~ keeps every primitive ring. sI and sII are
orthogonal, and the default window leaves their census unchanged.

#+begin_src bash
seams cages input/traj/genice_sI.lammpstrj --type 1 --graph cutoff --signature 512+51262
seams cages input/traj/genice_sII.lammpstrj --type 1 --graph cutoff --signature 512+51264
seams cages input/traj/genice_sH.lammpstrj --type 1 --graph cutoff \
  --cage-edge none --signature 512+sh+51268
#+end_src

The lines you should see are ~cages 2~ and ~cages 6~ on sI, ~cages 16~
and ~cages 8~ on sII, and ~cages 12~, ~cages 8~ and ~cages 4~ on sH.
