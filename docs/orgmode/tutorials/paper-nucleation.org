#+TITLE: Ice-likeness along a short nucleation run
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

Ice-likeness here is the mean Lechner-Dellago \(\bar q_6\). A liquid
sits near 0.2. A crystal sits near 0.5. A nucleus in a liquid pulls
the mean up as it grows, which is why the score is the whole-frame
average and not a single molecule.

The trajectory has three frames and is built in the test. GenIce ice
Ih is shaken into a liquid. The basal half of the original lattice is
the nucleus. Frame 0 is the shaken liquid. Frame 1 puts half of that
nucleus back on its lattice sites. Frame 2 puts the rest back. OpenMM
relaxes each frame for two steps with those sites restrained, so the
liquid can move and the nucleus cannot dissolve before you measure it.

~meson test docs_paper_nucleation~ checks

#+begin_src text
nucleation frame 0 q6bar 0.276023
nucleation frame 1 q6bar 0.320504
nucleation frame 2 q6bar 0.334254
#+end_src

The mean rises at each stage. OVITO draws the last frame. The test
exits 77 when GenIce, OpenMM, or OVITO is absent.

* Full run

The ice threshold on the mean Lechner-Dellago \(\bar q_6\) is 0.36.
The fast run finishes at 0.334254, still on the liquid side of that
cut, because the restored nucleus is only the basal half of 64
waters. =snakemake -s docs/tutorials/Snakefile nucleation= uses a 4
by 4 by 4 ice Ih cell, 1024 molecules. Frame 0 shakes every oxygen by
up to 1.6 Å. Frame 1 puts 256 basal molecules back on the lattice.
Frame 2 puts all 1024 back. OpenMM on the Reference platform
minimises each frame, and the restored sites are restrained so the
nucleus cannot dissolve before you measure it.

=meson test --suite slow docs_paper_nucleation_full= checks

#+begin_src text
nucleation full frame 0 q6bar 0.26828
nucleation full frame 1 q6bar 0.287644
nucleation full frame 2 q6bar 0.56248
#+end_src

Frames 0 and 1 stay below 0.36. Frame 2 is 0.56248, above the ice
threshold. The mean crosses the cut when the cell is crystalline
again. OVITO draws that last frame. The test exits 77 when GenIce,
OpenMM, OVITO, or Snakemake is absent.
