#+TITLE: Ice-water interface
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

One frame of a basal interface is ~input/traj/interface_f0.lammpstrj~,
864 molecules, 35 kB. ~meson test docs_tutorial_interface~ labels it
with CHILL+ and reads the profile along the long box edge.

The published rule on the cutoff list prints

#+begin_src text
hexagonal 352 water 434
stack HHHHHHHH....C.
#+end_src

The stack is eight hexagonal slabs, a gap, and one cubic layer. Cubic
ice is one molecule of the 864. \(\phi_c\), the cubic fraction of the
crystal molecules, is about 0.003.

Slabs are 3.7 Å along \(z\). The ice fraction is 0.95 in the slab that
starts at 18.5 Å and 0.48 in the next one. The front is the height
where that fraction crosses one half, 22.03 Å. Above it the slabs are
liquid, apart from one crystal molecule near 44 Å.

#+begin_src bash
seams chill-plus input/traj/interface_f0.lammpstrj --type 1 --layers --axis 2
#+end_src
