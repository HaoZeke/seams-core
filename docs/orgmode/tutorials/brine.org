#+TITLE: Brine and NaCl
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

~input/traj/nacl_front.lammpstrj~ is the 864-molecule interface with
nine ions added. Type 1 is water. Types 3 and 4 are chloride and
sodium. One chloride sits in a slab that CHILL+ calls fully
crystalline. The other eight ions sit in liquid slabs.
~meson test docs_tutorial_brine~ checks that split.

CHILL+ is run on the water alone, so an ion is not given an ice
label. Each ion is then counted in the 3.7 Å slab that contains it.
A slab is ice when at least half of its water is cubic, hexagonal,
reCubic or reHex.

| slab | ions | side |
|------+------+------|
| 3 | 1 | ice |
| 9 | 2 | liquid |
| 10 | 2 | liquid |
| 12 | 2 | liquid |
| 13 | 2 | liquid |

One ion is on the ice side of the front and eight are on the liquid
side. The water census is unchanged from the ion-free frame:
hexagonal 352.
