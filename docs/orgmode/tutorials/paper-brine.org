#+TITLE: Ice growing into brine
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

The front is the height where the crystal slabs stop. CHILL+ labels
the waters. Slabs of 3.7 Å along z are written as a stack: ~H~ for
hexagonal, ~C~ for cubic, ~.~ for a slab that is neither. The front
is the first slab that is not crystal, times 3.7 Å. Ions are not
waters, so they stay out of that count. You still want them in the
box, because the liquid they sit in is the brine the ice is growing
into.

Packmol places six waters, one sodium and one chloride above a fixed
GenIce ice Ih block. OpenMM then relaxes three stages. At each later
stage the nearest brine oxygen is moved onto the next lattice plane
and restrained. That move is the growth step. The relax is why the
ions and the leftover liquid can answer it, instead of staying where
packmol left them.

~meson test docs_paper_brine~ checks

#+begin_src text
brine frame 0 hexagonal 31 water 38 front 7.40
brine frame 1 hexagonal 32 water 36 front 7.40
brine frame 2 hexagonal 32 water 35 front 7.40
#+end_src

The front stays at 7.40 Å. The stack remains ~HH...~, so the new
crystal has not filled another 3.7 Å slab. The hexagonal count still
rises from 31 to 32, and the water count falls from 38 to 35. One
molecule has joined the ice. OVITO draws the last frame. The test
exits 77 when GenIce, packmol, OpenMM, or OVITO is absent.

* Full run

One new hexagonal molecule does not fill a 3.7 Å slab, which is why
the fast front never leaves 7.40 Å. The full run gives each stage a
whole plane. =snakemake -s docs/tutorials/Snakefile brine= builds a
2 by 2 by 2 ice Ih block, 128 waters, and two liquid planes of 64
waters seated a fraction of an angstrom off the next lattice sites.
Two sodium ions and two chloride ions sit above those planes. The ice
block stays restrained. Langevin dynamics run at 240 K on the
Reference platform, seed 240, friction 5 per ps, step 0.001 ps. Each
later stage turns on the spring field of the next plane, minimises,
and integrates 800 steps. The field is the undercooling that seats
that plane at 240 K. The run is long enough for the front to move.

=meson test --suite slow docs_paper_brine_full= checks

#+begin_src text
brine full frame 0 hexagonal 93 water 152 front 18.50 stack HHHHH.......
brine full frame 1 hexagonal 160 water 86 front 25.90 stack HHHHHHH.....
brine full frame 2 hexagonal 218 water 38 front 37.00 stack HHHHHHHHHH..
#+end_src

The front moves from 18.50 Å to 25.90 Å, a gain of one slab, and then
to 37.00 Å. The stack grows from ~HHHHH.......~ to ~HHHHHHH.....~ and
then to ~HHHHHHHHHH..~. Hexagonal waters rise from 93 to 218, and
liquid waters fall from 152 to 38. OVITO draws the last frame. The
test exits 77 when GenIce, OpenMM, OVITO, or Snakemake is absent.
