CHILL, CHILL+, the shell match, rings with symmetry codes, cages, prisms, the monolayer ring census and the 2D RDF scale with OpenMP threads. The kNN graph rows, cell sorts, ring-pool merge, cage sweep flags, prism ring index and frame-handle label copies run on the team, and the frame handle keeps its frame copy between calls. On 110592-molecule ice Ic in `bench/omp-scaling`, 8 threads run CHILL+ in 6.1 ms instead of 12.2, rings with symmetry codes in 89 ms instead of 415, cages in 68 ms instead of 121 and prisms in 51 ms instead of 163, 4.2 to 6.9 times faster than 1 thread, and the 1-thread times are no slower than before. Every result is identical at 1, 2, 4 and 8 threads.
