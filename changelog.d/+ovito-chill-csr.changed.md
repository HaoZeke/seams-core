OVITO-rule cutoff CHILL+ (`seams_chill_plus` with `SEAMS_CHILL_OVITO`, and `seams_frame_chill_plus_rule` with `ovito-chill+`) reads the in-tree cutoff CSR at every thread count, as the published rule already did. One pass sorts each shell and sums q3 into flat arrays, and a second pass classifies the bonds. It no longer depends on the optional linkcell list at one thread, or on vesin when linkcell is not built. On 110592-molecule ice Ic at one thread the call is about twice as fast, and labels and c_ij are unchanged. A language frame whose atom ids repeat keeps the row path.
