# Liquid series

`input/traj/dump-l.lammpstrj` frame 1 is a 512-molecule liquid in reduced units (nearest neighbour about 1.05, box about 11.8). Lengths are multiplied by 7/3, so the campaign cutoff of 3.5 Å sits at reduced radius 1.5, where the seed's mean coordination is about 4.2 and the neighbour counts run from 0 through 9. Replicas are 2×1×1 (1024), 3×3×2 (9216), 4×4×6 (49152) and 6×6×6 (110592). Unlike ice Ic, where every molecule has 4 neighbours and every primitive ring is a hexagon, these frames carry a spread of neighbour counts and primitive rings of sizes 3, 4, 5 and 6, and that histogram scales with the replica. d-SEAMS, freud 3.6.1 and OVITO 3.17.2 agree on the neighbour-count histogram; d-SEAMS and OVITO agree on the ring-size histogram. On the 110592-molecule frame the medians are 48.89 ms (d-SEAMS), 121.24 ms (freud) and 57.75 ms (OVITO) for neighbours, and 192.63 ms (d-SEAMS) and 830.13 ms (OVITO) for rings. The run is `PYTHONPATH=.h2h-py python3.12 scripts/h2h_cpc.py run all` on one core (`taskset -c 0`, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`, `OVITO_THREAD_COUNT=1`, freud threads 1), release build with g++ 15.2.0, Ubuntu 24.04.4, `Model name:                              Intel(R) Xeon(R) Processor`, 8 cores, numpy 2.5.4 and scipy 1.18.1. `h2h-cpc/data/exact-cutoff-result.txt` is the equal-cutoff measurement record, kept as data.
