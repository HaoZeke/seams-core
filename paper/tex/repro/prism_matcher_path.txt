Prism numbers in the paper do not use the shape-matcher early return.

Call path for the worked-example prism counts
paper/tex/figures/worked_example.py calls seams_frame_prisms.
bindings/cxx/seams_lang.cpp passes doShapeMatching false into
ring::findPrisms (the false argument on the findPrisms call).
src/topo_one_dim.cpp calls match::matchPrism only inside the
doShapeMatching branches. With that flag false, strict pairs are
counted without a match, and relaxed pairs are skipped.
src/topo_bulk.cpp leaves its match::matchPrism call commented out.

Ring sizes
paper/tex/figures/worked_example.py and stats.py set PRISM_SIZES
to 4, 5 and 6. The tubes are square, pentagonal and hexagonal.
No paper source mentions a heptagonal prism. The table in
paper/tex/rg_main.tex reports prism4, prism5 and prism6 only.
The RMSD number in the supplement is the OVITO polyhedral-template
cutoff, not a prism-match RMSD.

Shape matcher
paper/tex/repro/build.sh configures -Dwith_ira=disabled.
Without that library, ira::match returns 1 and ira::orient returns
false, so matchPrismBlock does not take the early return.
The pentagonal ring is size 5, which would be the affected odd case
if the shape matcher were linked and shape matching were requested.
Neither is true for the paper build.

No paper result, figure, table or supplement number was regenerated.
Old and new values are the same because that path was not run.
